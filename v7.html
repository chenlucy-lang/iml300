Alt-Text as Poetry

I was inspired by Coklyat and Finnegan’s Alt-Text as Poetry reading. Alt-text is central to web accessibility, yet it’s often relegated to a mere compliance checkbox or a chore on the developer’s end. But through engaging with the text, I admired how the project reframes it as an expressive act, one that also raises awareness of the experiences of blind and low-vision people. In response, I built a website where hovering over an image reveals a poem in the form of hypertext. This invites exploration while drawing attention to hypertext itself as a mode of communication.
