Project 1: Interactive Audio & Visual with p5.js

p5.js, Interactive Code, Audiovisual

Inspired by Anaïs Nin’s idea of experiencing life twice, once in the moment and again in retrospect, this project explores the relationship between photography, memory, and recollection through the nostalgic medium of cassette tapes. While photographs preserve moments in time, our memories often distort them into fragmented impressions of colors, sounds, and emotions. Through revisiting places from my past year, I wanted to capture not necessarily what these experiences looked like, but how they felt.

Project 2: Hypernarrative

Narrative-based Game, Illustration Art

This project is an interactive, illustrated choose-your-own-adventure based on Ursula K. Le Guin’s short story “The Ones Who Walk Away from Omelas.” Omelas is a gleaming utopia, a city of festivals and abundance that needs no kings, knights, or laws to keep its people happy. But can a place like that really exist? Beneath the splendor lies a terrible price. When you discover what it costs to sustain this joy, what will you do: be complacent or walk away?
